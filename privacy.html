Privacy Policy — Innosafer

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy describes how Innosafer ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our website and our Pinterest application ("Innosafer Pin Publisher").

1. What we do

Innosafer curates home & kitchen products from Amazon.de and publishes product pins to our own Pinterest business account. Our Pinterest application is used solely to publish content to our own Pinterest account; it does not access, collect or process data from any other Pinterest user.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal data from visitors of this website or from Pinterest users. The Pinterest application accesses only our own account via the official Pinterest API (scopes: boards and pins read/write) and uses the access token exclusively for our own publishing.

3. Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate (Amazon.de PartnerNet), we earn from qualifying purchases. Product links published by Innosafer are affiliate links and are marked as advertising ("Anzeige/Werbung").

4. Third-party services

We link to Amazon.de and Pinterest. These platforms have their own privacy policies, which apply when you visit them.

5. Contact

For questions about this policy or the application, contact us via our Pinterest profile or at the email address registered with our Pinterest developer account.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this URL.
